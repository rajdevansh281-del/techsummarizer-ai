DEPLOY TO YOUR EXISTING HUGGING FACE SPACE

Existing Space:
https://huggingface.co/spaces/DEVANSH0000000/techsummarizer-ai

1. Open the Space and select the Files tab.
2. Upload these three files from this folder to the ROOT of the Space:
   - app.py
   - requirements.txt
   - README.md
3. If asked whether to overwrite README.md, choose replace/commit the new version.
4. Commit the changes. Hugging Face will build and restart the Space automatically.
5. Open the Logs tab and wait for the model download and the message:
   "TechSummarizer AI model loaded."
6. Open the App tab and test Text, PDF, and Website URL.

IMPORTANT HARDWARE NOTE
This app is prepared for a Hugging Face ZeroGPU Space. In the Space Settings, select ZeroGPU if your account/Space is eligible. The app uses spaces.GPU around model inference. If ZeroGPU is not available, do not randomly change package versions; share the Space build/runtime logs so the app can be adapted to the hardware you can use.

PERMANENT-LINK NOTE
The Hugging Face Space URL is a persistent address; it does not mean the app runs 24/7. Free Spaces can sleep when inactive, and free ZeroGPU has daily usage quotas. Visitors can wake a sleeping Space by opening it, but startup can take time.

SECURITY / PRIVACY
A public Space is accessible to others. Do not upload private or confidential PDFs. Anyone with access to a public Space can use its app. Do not put account passwords or access tokens in app.py or README.md.
